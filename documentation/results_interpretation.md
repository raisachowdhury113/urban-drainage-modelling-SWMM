# Baseline results interpretation

## Hydrologic continuity
The RPT reports total precipitation of 142.000 mm, surface runoff of 130.574 mm, infiltration loss of 1.226 mm, and runoff continuity error of -0.346%.

## Hydraulic continuity and flooding
The RPT reports wet-weather inflow of 19.431 million litres, flooding loss of 17.537 million litres, final stored volume of 1.853 million litres, and flow-routing continuity error of +0.215%.

The node flooding summary identifies J2, J3, J7, J11, J29, J45, J22 and J39. J11 has the largest reported flood volume (7.034 million litres).

## Numerical stability
The RPT reports 6.69% nonconverging steps and high local continuity errors at J27, J24, J7, J31 and J3. These require review before detailed hydraulic conclusions.

## LID outputs
For each LID-treated subcatchment, the report gives 142.00 mm total inflow, 115.31 mm drain outflow, 12.00 mm initial storage and 39.06 mm final storage. These are LID-unit results, not a before/after catchment-wide comparison.

## Interpretation
Continuity percentages indicate overall water-balance closure. They do not demonstrate sufficient drainage capacity. Flooding, nonconverging steps, local continuity errors, and the inactive storage/pump/outfall pathway are important interpretation considerations.
